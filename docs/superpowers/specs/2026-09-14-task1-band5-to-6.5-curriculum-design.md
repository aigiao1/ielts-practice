# Task 1 Band 5 to Band 6.5 Archetype-Driven Curriculum & Expanded Scaffolds Design

## 1. Context & User Need
- **User Pain Point**: The previous Task 1 workbench suffered from severe repetition across question types. Charts only had 3–4 trivial numbers (e.g. 45%, 30%, 15%, 10%), which made genuine comparison, grouping, and overview writing impossible. All question types (even maps and processes) displayed the same static percentage phrases (`accounted for the lion's share`).
- **Core Goal**: 
  1. Cover **ALL 6 IELTS Task 1 Question Types** (Static Pie/Bar, Dynamic Line/Trend, Comparative Grouped Bar/Table, Map Transformation, Process Flow, Mixed Dual Charts).
  2. Structure a **1-Month (28-day, 1 group/day) progressive roadmap** systematically designed to lift a test taker from **Band 5 to Band 6.5**.
  3. Expand chart data to **5–6 clean, realistic data points** per prompt (simple and readable, yet with sufficient material for meaningful groupings, comparisons, and multipliers).
  4. Implement **Type-Adaptive Chunk Drawers** and **Type-Specific Dual Thinking Angles** that completely eradicate formulaic repetition.

## 2. Band 5 vs Band 6.5 Diagnostic Rubric

| Assessment Criteria | Band 5 Traps (Where Learners Get Stuck) | Band 6.5 Standards (Our Guaranteed Target) | System Implementation & Guardrails |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task Achievement (TA)** | Merely lists isolated numbers ("Housing was 45%. Food was 30%..."). No overview, or overview contains trivial data. | Presents a clear, data-free **Overview** highlighting macro trends/dominant features. Appropriately groups data into 2 distinct Body paragraphs. | **Anti-Band-5 Overview Rule**: Overview step explicitly guides macro patterns without isolated numbers. Dual thinking angles force 2 distinct grouping logics. |
| **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)** | Inadequate or mechanical paragraphing. Overuses `firstly, secondly, and, also`. | Well-organized 4-paragraph structure (`Intro ➔ Overview ➔ Body 1 ➔ Body 2`). Cohesive devices used effectively with varied references. | **4-Step Progressive Stepper**: Step-by-step drafting prevents stream-of-consciousness writing and produces a clean 140–165 word synthesis. |
| **Lexical Resource (LR)** | Formulaic repetition. Uses static percentage phrases (`accounted for`) on dynamic lines, maps, and processes. Frequent spelling errors. | Uses a wide range of task-specific academic collocations: trend verbs for lines, spatial/demolition verbs for maps, passive sequencing for processes. | **Type-Adaptive Chunk Drawer**: Automatically swaps the right-side chunk drawer based on active chart archetype (Trend, Map, Process, Static, Comparison). |
| **Grammatical Range (GRA)** | Relies exclusively on simple sentences (`A was 35%. B was 25%`). Struggles with passive voice and complex comparisons. | Mix of complex sentences (while/whereas, participle clauses, passive voice for maps/processes, proportionate multipliers). | **Structured Sentence Skeletons**: Provides grammatical templates with clear bracket placeholders for high-band structures. |

## 3. The 6 Archetype Matrix & Curriculum Mapping

The 7 modules (28 groups) map to the 4-week, 28-day training curriculum:

### Archetype 1: 静态占比与顺位图 (Static Proportions & Rankings)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 1 (Groups 1–4), Module 3 (Groups 9–12)
- **Chart Data**: 5–6 categories totaling 100% (e.g. Housing 35%, Food 25%, Transport 15%, Healthcare 12%, Recreation 8%, Clothing 5%).
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (Ranking Magnitude)`: First-tier leaders (top 2 = 60%) vs secondary mid-tier & tail items.
  - `Angle B (Functional Nature)`: Essential living expenses (housing & food) vs discretionary/flexible spending.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Proportions: `accounted for the lion's share of`, `represented approximately one-third`, `constituted a mere 5%`.
  - Multipliers & Margins: `outnumbered ... by two to one`, `was seven times as high as`, `surpassed ... by a narrow margin of`.

### Archetype 2: 动态走势与反超图 (Dynamic Trends & Trajectories)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 2 (Groups 5–8)
- **Chart Data**: 3 distinct trajectories across 4 time periods (1990, 2000, 2010, 2020), exhibiting steady growth, precipitous drops, plateaus, and an intersection/overtake.
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (By Trajectory Pattern)`: Upward-trending series grouped together vs declining/fluctuating series.
  - `Angle B (Chronological Milestones)`: Initial positions & mid-period development vs final phase divergence & overtaking.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Movement: `experienced a sustained upward trajectory`, `witnessed a precipitous decline`, `plummeted to an all-time low`.
  - Milestones: `peaked at [N] before leveling off`, `overtook [A] to become the predominant ...`, `fluctuated marginally around`.

### Archetype 3: 多主体横向对比 (Comparative Categories & Grouped Bars)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 4 (Groups 13–16)
- **Chart Data**: 2–3 subjects (e.g. UK vs US, or Male vs Female) compared across 4–5 key sectors.
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (By Subject Matchup)`: Subject A's overall profile vs Subject B's comprehensive performance.
  - `Angle B (By Performance Dimension)`: Areas of mutual parity/superiority vs areas of glaring contrast.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Comparison: `consistently outperformed`, `lagged significantly behind`, `showed a contrasting profile`.
  - Disparity: `was virtually on par with`, `in sharp contrast to`, `exhibited the most pronounced disparity in`.

### Archetype 4: 地图演变与空间重构 (Map Transformation - Past vs Present)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 5 (Groups 17–20)
- **Chart Data**: Town/campus transformation showing 5–6 clear spatial alterations (farmland converted to residential blocks, path widened into a dual carriageway, old docks replaced by marina, factory relocated to eastern outskirts, new railway station erected).
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (By Cardinal Zones)`: Northern waterfront & town center redevelopment vs Southern rural & industrial expansion.
  - `Angle B (By Redevelopment Nature)`: Facilities demolished/repurposed vs brand-new modern infrastructure introduced.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Demolition & Modernization: `was pulled down to make way for`, `underwent comprehensive redevelopment`, `was repurposed into`.
  - Construction & Spatial: `witnessed the construction of`, `situated directly to the north of`, `flanked by`, `expanded eastward`.

### Archetype 5: 流程工序与被动衔接 (Process Diagram - Stages & Cycles)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 6 (Groups 21–24)
- **Chart Data**: 5–6 sequential stages with inputs, physical transformations, and finished outputs (e.g. Coffee processing: Harvesting ➔ Drying/Sorting ➔ Roasting ➔ Grinding ➔ Vacuum Packaging/Export).
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (Sequential Phases)`: Primary raw material extraction & preparation vs secondary refining, packaging & dispatch.
  - `Angle B (Material State)`: Solid-phase mechanical handling vs thermal/chemical transformation.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Stage Initiation: `The process commences with the harvesting of...`, `is initially sorted and cleaned`.
  - Transformation & Passives: `is subsequently fed into a cylindrical drum`, `is subjected to intense heating`, `undergoes rigorous filtration`.
  - Culmination: `culminates in the automated packing of...`, `is dispatched to retail outlets`.

### Archetype 6: 综合图与高分实战 (Mixed / Dual Charts)
- **Applicable Modules**: Module 7 (Groups 25–28)
- **Chart Data**: Dual-source data (e.g. Pie chart of general energy consumption combined with a Bar chart of per-capita emissions across 5 nations).
- **Thinking Angles**:
  - `Angle A (Cross-Chart Macro Correlation)`: Overview linking macro consumption to specific national emissions, followed by Chart 1 breakdown in Body 1 and Chart 2 breakdown in Body 2.
  - `Angle B (Categorical Synthesis)`: Grouping heavy-carbon sectors/countries in Body 1 vs low-carbon/renewable sectors in Body 2.
- **Dedicated Chunk Drawer**:
  - Synthesis: `Taking both charts into consideration, ...`, `A closer inspection of the accompanying bar chart reveals ...`, `corresponded directly to`.

## 4. 28-Day Band 5 to 6.5 Study Schedule

- **Week 1 (Days 1–7): Eliminate Listing Habits & Master Static Grouping**
  - Focus: Eliminating mechanical "A was 35%, B was 25%". Master the data-free Overview and 2-angle grouping on static charts.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14): Dynamic Movement, Intersections & Comparative Tables**
  - Focus: Dynamic verbs, trajectory modeling, peak/trough identification, and cross-entity table comparisons.
- **Week 3 (Days 15–21): Maps & Processes Specialization**
  - Focus: Complete eradication of percentage vocab on maps/processes. Fluency with passive voice, spatial prepositions, and step connectives.
- **Week 4 (Days 22–28): Synthesis, Dual Charts & Band 6.5 Full Timed Drills**
  - Focus: Multi-source synthesis, nuanced transitions, and achieving 150-word balanced essays in under 20 minutes.
